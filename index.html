Reading Between the Lesions

Accepted · MICCAI 2026 · Mechanistic AI Safety for Medical Foundation Models

Auditing whether multimodal dermatology classifiers actually use clinical text

High multimodal accuracy is often treated as evidence of grounded reasoning. I test that claim directly with matched counterfactual probes, ablations, activation patching, Top-K sparse autoencoders on fused representations, and foundation-model escalation to LLaVA-Med.

  • Semantic grounding fails the margin test. Aligned clinical text decreased the correct-class margin (−0.0102 mean shift), not improved it.
  • Text presence ≠ text meaning. Ablation probes and shuffle controls expose reliance on lexical scaffolding over clinical semantics.
  • Mechanistic shortcut features. SAE Feature 1449 behaves like a peripheral-context sensitivity probe, not semantic integration.
  • Foundation-model collapse. LLaVA-Med shows 0% prediction flips under explicitly contradictory histories—consistent with pretraining priors dominating integration.

Why this is AI safety work

The failure mode is general: systems can appear multimodally competent while failing basic counterfactual update tests. That gap between performance and mechanism is exactly what makes premature deployment dangerous.

Lesion-disjoint leakage audits Matched 2×2 grounding controls Neutral / blank / contradictory probes SAE-based shortcut discovery LVLM counterfactual traps

Dermatology is the testbed. The target is a reusable audit stack for vision-language systems in high-stakes settings.

Research program

Mechanistic audits before deployment

01

Behavioural grounding tests

Matched counterfactual designs, blank/neutral/shuffled text probes, and flip-rate metrics that separate apparent multimodal gains from genuine semantic integration.

02

Internal mechanism tracing

Activation patching, residual-stream SAEs, and causal interventions to identify shortcut circuits and test whether internal features can be manipulated interpretably.

03

Foundation-model escalation

Escalating audits from discriminative fusion models to instruction-tuned LVLMs, including steering tests and syntactic-prior collapse diagnostics.

04

Deployment-blocking reporting

A minimal checklist—leakage controls, tabular baselines, subgroup reliability, shortcut reporting—so aggregate AUROC cannot hide mechanism failure.
