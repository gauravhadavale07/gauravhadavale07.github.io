About & Research Interests

I am a final-year B.Tech student at the University of Mumbai, currently interning at A*STAR Singapore, driven by a singular goal: ensuring that as artificial intelligence systems scale in power and capability, they remain fundamentally safe, interpretable, and aligned with human values.

Why AI Safety Matters: We are witnessing an unprecedented acceleration in AI capabilities. Frontier models are rapidly matching and exceeding human performance across complex domains. However, society remains dangerously unprepared for the risks these systems pose. When models are deployed as "black boxes," their high accuracy on benchmarks often masks dangerous failure modes, such as relying on superficial shortcuts rather than genuine reasoning. In high-stakes settings like healthcare, mistaken confidence driven by these uninterpretable shortcuts can lead to catastrophic, systemic failures. The gap between how fast AI is advancing and how fast the safety workforce is growing is the most dangerous mismatch of our time.

My Approach: My research directly targets this gap by moving beyond standard accuracy metrics. I focus on Technical AI Safety and Mechanistic Interpretability—opening the black box to understand how models make decisions. Instead of trusting a model because it achieves high scores, I build rigorous, mechanistic evaluations and algorithmic audits to probe for semantic grounding failures. By designing matched counterfactual probes, performing causal interventions on model activations, and using tools like Sparse Autoencoders (SAEs), I aim to make failure modes legible before models reach deployment.

How My Projects Tackle This: My current work interrogates whether multimodal foundation models (LVLMs) actually use the reasoning pathways they appear to use. I demonstrate that models with high accuracy are often collapsing to pre-training priors and tokenizer scaffolding rather than genuinely synthesizing visual and textual evidence. By identifying these shortcut mechanisms at the activation layer, my research provides the necessary auditing tools to block unsafe models from deployment and guides the development of genuinely robust systems.
